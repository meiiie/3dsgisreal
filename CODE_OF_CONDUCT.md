# Code Of Conduct

3DGS Real should be a focused, respectful engineering space.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, kind, and specific.
- Critique code, design, and reasoning, not people.
- Respect privacy around real places, rooms, students, operators, and captured media.
- Prefer evidence and reproducible checks over authority or taste fights.
- Help keep the project usable for new contributors.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or repeated hostile comments.
- Publishing private capture data, private locations, credentials, or personal information.
- Pressuring maintainers to accept unverified architecture, security, or UX changes.
- Spam, off-topic promotion, or low-effort generated contributions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, close, or remove issues, comments, pull requests, or contributions that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in a ban from the repository.

If you see a problem, contact the maintainers through a private GitHub channel when available.
